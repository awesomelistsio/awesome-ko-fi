> A curated list of awesome tools, resources, guides, and integrations for using Ko-fi.

[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com) is a platform that allows creators, developers, and organizations to receive donations, memberships, and sell products with minimal fees. This list helps you make the most out of Ko-fi as a creator or developer.

## Official Resources

- [Ko-fi Official Website](https://ko-fi.com) – Home page and creator setup.
- [Ko-fi Blog](https://blog.ko-fi.com) – Updates, creator tips, and platform news.
- [Ko-fi Help Center](https://ko-fi.com/help) – Official support and FAQs.
- [Ko-fi Developer API](https://developers.ko-fi.com/) – Documentation for integrating Ko-fi features into your apps and websites.

## Guides & Tutorials

- [How to Set Up Your Ko-fi Page](https://blog.ko-fi.com/how-to-set-up-your-ko-fi-page) – Step-by-step guide from Ko-fi.
- [Earning on Ko-fi](https://blog.ko-fi.com/earning-on-ko-fi) – Strategies for maximizing support and sales.
- [Using Ko-fi Widgets](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004831597-How-to-Add-Ko-fi-Widgets-to-Your-Site) – Embedding donation buttons and shop widgets.

## Integrations & Tools

- [Ko-fi WordPress Plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/ko-fi-button/) – Add Ko-fi buttons and widgets easily on WordPress sites.
- [Zapier – Ko-fi Integrations](https://zapier.com/apps/ko-fi/integrations) – Automate workflows between Ko-fi and other apps.
- [IFTTT Ko-fi Automation](https://ifttt.com/search/query/ko-fi) – Community-made applets using Ko-fi (limited availability).
- [Ko-fi Link Shorteners](https://ko-fi.com/links) – Create custom shortened links for your Ko-fi page.

## Themes & Embeds

- [Ko-fi Button Generator](https://ko-fi.com/buttons) – Official button customization tool.
- [Custom Ko-fi Widgets via HTML/CSS](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004831597-How-to-Add-Ko-fi-Widgets-to-Your-Site) – Embed donation buttons, progress bars, and galleries.
- [Ko-fi Stream Alerts for OBS](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004827657-Ko-fi-Stream-Alerts) – Set up Ko-fi donations as stream alerts.

## Communities & Forums

- [r/KoFi on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/KoFi/) – Community support, questions, and tips.
- [Ko-fi Discord Server](https://discord.com/invite/kofi) – Official Ko-fi creator community (check Ko-fi for invite link if available).
- [Twitter: @KoFi](https://twitter.com/KoFi) – Official updates and announcements.

## Alternative Platforms

- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com) – Popular membership and creator support platform.
- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/) – Creator support with similar features to Ko-fi.
- [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com/) – Open-source recurring donations platform.
